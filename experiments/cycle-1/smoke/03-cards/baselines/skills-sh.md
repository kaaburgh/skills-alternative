## Present in the native record, referenced rather than reproduced

The listing also renders the author's skill name and description, the trigger list, and quick-start
command examples. Those are the skill author's content. skills.sh states it does not own, host or
relicense skill content, and the upstream repository carries no licence, so this experiment
references them at the native coordinate and at upstream commit `2a0e09d8` instead of copying them.

**This is a real limit on the baseline, not an omission of convenience.** A baseline may not drop
awkward content, and nothing has been dropped for being awkward — but "complete" here means
field-complete by reference, not verbatim reproduction. `SA-003` has to settle whether the
comparator-completeness contract is satisfied by reference, because for any unlicensed upstream it
cannot be satisfied any other way.
